## Hidden/double-meaning logo precedents

- FedEx's embedded arrow (Landor, 1994) is the canonical "reward on second
  look"; Amazon's a→z smile, Toblerone's bear, Baskin-Robbins' "31" all
  work the same way: a **primary reading that never fails at small size**,
  plus a hidden layer that only appears once you look. Design rule taken
  from the family: the hidden layer must share strokes with the primary
  form, never add parts that hurt legibility.
- Round-1 feedback applied: v1-C1's ECG tail was too small — so in v2 the
  *hidden* element is always at main-stroke scale, and the primary reading
  is a letterform or bold silhouette.

## Physiological sources for the new concepts

- **Vectorcardiogram (VCG)**: the cardiac dipole tip traces three spatial
  loops per beat — small **P loop**, large **QRS loop**, medium **T loop**.
  The QRS loop is an elongated, slightly teardrop-shaped loop — visually a
  narrow "leaf" — and is a genuinely niche cardiology reference.
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9536877/ ,
  https://www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.1515/bmt-2015-0005/html?lang=en
- **Respiratory sinus arrhythmia (RSA)**: heart rate rises on inspiration,
  falls on expiration — the RR interval literally breathes. That justifies
  one-stroke concepts where a slow respiration wave *carries* the fast ECG
  (two vitals, one line). Eckberg 2003 review + 2025 expert recommendation
  to rename it "respiratory heart rate variability":
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6853303/ ,
  https://www.nature.com/articles/s41569-025-01160-z ,
  https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/10.1161/01.CIR.94.4.842
- **Sampling**: digitisation = dots at sample instants; quantified-signal
  language (PPG ADC sampling refs from v1 research apply).

## Concept seeds → hidden message

| Concept | Primary read | Hidden layer |
|---|---|---|
| c1_rwave_v | bold **V** | right stroke overshoots into the R spike — "the name starts with a heartbeat" |
| c2_qcounter | bold **Q** | the tail is the same trace that spikes *inside* the counter — a pulse hiding in the hole |
| c3_qdots | PQRST trace | the trace is made of discrete sample dots — "life, measured" |
| c4_breath | slow wave | its crest is a QRS spike — respiration and heartbeat in one stroke (RSA) |
| c5_vcg | bold **Q** | the bowl is a VCG QRS vector-loop (teardrop, E-point cusp) |
| c6_hub | chest trace | the line terminates into radiating arcs — from skin to clinician |
| c7_tittle | torso arch + sternum dot | in the lockup that dot *is* the tittle of the "i" |

All marks keep v1 geometry rules: ≤2 stroke weights, no fine detail under
~3 % of mark size, embossable as a single-relief layer, mono/reversed
variants carry no colour-only meaning.
