This is a work-in-progress backup of the VitalQ upgrade from the ESP32-S3-MINI-1 to the ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N16R8.

**This board is NOT for ordering.** It currently fails DRC: about 1,029 errors and 141 unconnected at 22:55 (UK time) on 19 Oct 2026.

- The order-ready board is `current/fix_v19` on the `vitalq-deliverables` branch.
- Any zip in `fix_v20/fab/` is stale and must not be used.
- `fix_v20/_work/` holds intermediate boards, scripts and DRC reports from the routing work.
- `PROMPT_DEVIN_WROOM_FREEROUTE.md` is the routing prompt in use at the time of the backup.

Research prototype only; not a medical device.
