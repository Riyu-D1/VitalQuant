## Abs-max, five states
- VIN 3.0–4.35 V<7 ✓; VOUT=5 V<7 ✓; EN driven by U19 (VCCP=+3V3, ≤3.3 V) vs VIN≥3 V — EN≤VIN+0.3 ✓; USB-only battery-absent: VIN=0, EN driven 3.3 V with VIN=0 → violates EN≤VIN+0.3 by ≤3.6 V? EN abs-max table is "−0.3…7 V" for EN on this DS revision (signal pins are 7 V tolerant), so OK; even under the stricter reading, U19's open-drain-capable P-port + R25 limits injection to µA.

## Reference design / passives
- L2 1.0 µH, CIN 10 µ @3.1, COUT 4.7 µ @2.1 — matches DS typical-application exactly.
- TX_5V_RAW → R114/R96 0 Ω splits to TX_5V (U16/U6/U22 LED anodes). C14 1µ+C89 10µ bulk at the load.
