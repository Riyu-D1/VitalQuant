## Absolute-max vs power states
AS7341 VDD max 2.2 V — this is the strictest rail limit on the board. +1V8 is from TPS7A2018 (fixed 1.8 V ±2%), max ~1.84 V ≤2.2 V in all states (USB-only, battery-only, flat [rail off], charging). Digital pins (SCL/SDA) abs-max 3.6 V; on 1V8 domain ≤1.8 V. LDR sees ≤3.3 V ≤3.6 V. Skin-fault: n/a (enclosed sensor).

| State | Exposure | Result |
|-------|----------|--------|
| USB only / charging | VDD 1.8 V, I2C 1.8 V | OK |
| Battery only | same | OK |
| Battery flat | rail off; I2C_1V8 pull-ups off with rail — no back-drive | OK |
| Skin-land | none | n/a |

## Sequencing / passives
- No sequencing constraints beyond VDD before I2C traffic (same LDO brings up both) — the 1V8 pull-ups are on the same rail so signals can never exceed VDD.
- C36 100 nF @1.44 mm local; +1V8 bulk C87 10 µF. R21/R22 2.2 kΩ vs DS I2C pull-up guidance OK.
- D10 white LED on LDR: forward current set by AFE/FW; D10 drops ~2.8 V from 3.3 V → sink sees ~0.5 V compliance — within LDR operating range.

## Findings
None. 8/8 pads verified; variant DLGM = 1.8 V part on 1.8 V rail, correct.
