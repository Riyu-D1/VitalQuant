## Abs-max, five states
- VDD=1.8 V<2.2 ✓; VLED=TX_5V 5 V<6 ✓; **all SPI/INT/CSB pins are 6 V-tolerant — direct 3.3 V drive from U1 (CS_MAX86178, SCLK, MOSI) is within abs-max even with no level shift** ✓. U26 provides the SDO 1V8→3V3 return path; the forward-direction pins need no shift.
- USB-only (no battery): VDD=0 while U1 drives CS/SCLK/MOSI at ≤3.3 V — pins rated to +6 V independent of VDD ✓ (verified: abs-max references GND_ANA, not VDD).
- Skin-fault: PD/LED pins connect only to U16 package pins — no external exposure path ✓.

## Reference design / passives
- VREF 1 µ @8.9 mm — DS wants it close; accepted (dense BGA cluster: U18 @3.3 mm, U6 @3.4, J8 @5.7 — no clear slot; documented).
- VDD 10 µ @8.1 + two 100 n @8.7 — same accepted-density justification; +1V8 decap bulk exists at the LDO (C6 @LDO) and at U22's own 10 µ.
- R117 10 k on CS_MAX86178 (also U26.OE): both U22.CSB and U26.OE are ≥3.3 V-rated inputs — legal.

## Disagreements vs R5 audit
R5 flagged 3.3 V drive of the MAX86141 SPI/CS pins as a possible over-voltage on a 1V8 part. Re-check against the real DS this round: SCLK/SDO/SDI/CSB/INT are rated −0.3…+6.0 V — the 3.3 V drive is unconditionally legal. R5's concern resolved, no defect.
