## Abs-max, five states
- AVDD=DVDD=+3V3_ANA<5.25 ✓; all inputs ≤3.3 V ✓; ECG electrode inputs via 2.2n series + clamps (RLD_CLAMP net) — skin path protected; USB-only unpowered: electrode/RLD pins float, SPI hi-Z ✓.
- RLDOUT drives U6's ECG bias chain (5.11 MΩ each leg) — ≤3.3 V swing, within both parts' ranges ✓.

## Reference design / passives
- VCAP1/VCAP2/VREFP per DS exactly. RLD loop: R17 1 M + C65 1.5 n = 106 kHz loop pole, RLDREF 1 µ — matches TI RLD app guidance.
- Deviation (accepted): supply pins have only 100 n local; nearest 1 µ C80 @11.5 mm. +3V3_ANA is plane-fed and C28 covers HF; VCAPx handle the internal LDOs — measured-justified, no feasible slot in the dense AFE cluster (U8 ring: RLD_CLAMP vias <2.5 mm).

## Disagreements vs R5 audit
R5 recorded VREFP/VCAP as compliant and didn't quantify AVDD decap distance; now measured and justified. Functional conclusions concur.
