## Abs-max, five states
- USB-only: IN=5 V<30 ✓; OUT=VBAT cell 3–4.2 V ✓; logic ≤3.3 V<5.5 ✓.
- Battery-only (no USB): IN=0 — charger sleeps, body diode blocks; OUT 3–4.35 V<13 ✓.
- Charge/float: as above ✓.
- Battery-flat: VOUT≥0 ✓.
- Skin-fault: TS pin driven by pack NTC only ✓.

## Reference design / sequencing
EVM hookup identical (IN bypass, OUT bypass, ISET/VSET/TS resistors, OD pulls on STAT/PG). No sequencing requirements.

## Passives
- **R7 deviation FIXED**: previously nearest VBUS ≥1 µF was C10 1µ+C1 4.7µ @≈24.8 mm with only C63 100 n @8.6 mm at the pin — violated the DS "≥1 µF close to IC". C63 was relocated to (28.60,3.90), pad1 1.18 mm from U2.1, revalued to 1 µF (LCSC C14445 10 V X5R). C1 4.7µ+C10 1µ still absorb connector hot-plug transients at J1.
- R3 1.5 k ISET ≈ 600 mA; R75 27 k VSET = 4.2 V; both per DS tables.

## Disagreements vs R5 audit
R5 checked pin-out and programming but did not flag the IN-decap distance; this round measured it at 8.6 mm, fixed to 1.18 mm, regenerated Gerbers (Top Layer only).
