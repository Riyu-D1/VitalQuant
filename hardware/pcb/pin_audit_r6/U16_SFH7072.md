## Absolute-max vs power states
- LEDs: Vf green ~3.2 V / red ~2.1 V / IR ~1.5 V @ rated current; powered from TX_5V=5.0 V → headroom OK; reverse voltage rating ≥5 V not stressed (cathode sinks clamp low; when TX_5V off, both ends collapse).
- PDs: VR max 16 V — PD pins ride at AFE4900 RX-supply-domain levels ≪ limit.
- LED DC current limits (DS §3): G 25 mA, R 40 mA, IR 60 mA continuous; pulsed to 300 mA–1 A. Firmware must constrain DC drive — noted as FW requirement, not a copper defect (AFE4900 TX gain register).
- States: USB-only/battery-only/charging identical (TX_5V generated only on demand by U15 boost); battery-flat → TX_5V off; skin-fault → module faces skin optically, electrodes are on J5 (separate).

## Sequencing / passives
- TX_5V enabled via TX5_EN (U19.P2, R25 100k pull-down default-off) → LEDs dark at POR; AFE4900 held in PWDN simultaneously — consistent.
- C14 1 µF @1.4–3.1 mm + C89 10 µF @5 mm on TX_5V cover LED pulse current.
