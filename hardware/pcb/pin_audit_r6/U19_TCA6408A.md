## Absolute-max vs power states
- USB-only: VCCP=3.3; VBUS_DET=3.15 V even when VCCP=0 → **VI abs-max 6.5 V is VCC-independent (Ioff), legal** ✓. CHG_STAT ≤5 V same.
- Battery-only: VBUS_DET=0; OK.
- Charging/flat: bounded by dividers. Skin-fault n/a.

## Sequencing / passives
- VCCI=VCCP same rail — no ordering issue. /RESET held low until ESP boots → P-ports Hi-Z with the R-pull defaults listed (RESETZ low = AFE in PWDN — consistent with U6 sequencing).
- C59 100 nF shared @4.2/4.5 mm meets TI 0.1 µF guidance; pull-ups per DS.

## Findings
None. 16/16 pads verified; the two always-alive inputs (P5/P6) are legal per the 6.5 V / Ioff abs-max.
