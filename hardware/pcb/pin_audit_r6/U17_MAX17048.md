## Absolute-max vs power states
- USB-only / battery absent: CELL=VDD=0 V; SDA/SCL/ALRT at 3.3 V pull-ups → all ≤6 V ✓ (device unpowered, pins 6 V-rated — legal per abs-max table).
- Battery-only/charging: VBAT 3.0–4.35 V ≤4.5 V op / ≤12 V abs ✓.
- Battery-flat (<3.0 V): below 2.5 V operating min — device may read low SOC; not a rating violation (CELL abs 12 V).
- Skin-fault: n/a.

## Sequencing / passives
- None required. C3 1 µF + C58 100 nF within 2.1 mm exceed the 0.1 µF bypass spec.
- R74 10 kΩ ALRT pull-up per DS open-drain requirement; I2C pull-ups 2.2 kΩ fine.
