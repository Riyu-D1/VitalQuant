## Absolute-max vs power states
VDD/VDDIO abs −0.3…4.25 V; interface pins −0.3…VDDIO+0.3 V. All powered states keep VDD=VDDIO=3.3 V and SCK ≤3.3 V ≤ VDDIO+0.3 — marginal headroom: 3.3 V drive vs VDDIO+0.3=3.6 V → OK (0.3 V headroom). DS §7 note "no interface pin high while VDDIO off": pull-ups share VDDIO rail → satisfied automatically. Battery-flat: all off together. Skin-fault n/a.

## Sequencing / passives
- CSB tied to +3V3 = VDDIO → I2C selected before POR as required.
- C40 100 nF serves VDDIO(2.81 mm) and VDD(1.62 mm) — meets Bosch's 100 nF/pin recommendation via shared local cap (short common node). Acceptable.
- I2C on shared +3V3 bus: addr 0x76 unique.
