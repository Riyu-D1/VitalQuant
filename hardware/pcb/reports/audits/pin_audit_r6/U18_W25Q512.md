## Absolute-max vs power states
VCC max 4.6 V, I/O ≤VCC+0.4 V. All states: VCC=3.3 V, SPI=3.3 V → ≤3.7 V ✓. Battery-flat: all off; CS pull-up collapses with rail — no violation (pins go low with VCC). USB-only/charging identical. Skin-fault n/a.
Partial-power note: flash shares +3V3 with MCU — no domain crossing.

## Sequencing / passives
- /CS pulled high via R52 → device deselected through POR (DS §4/§7 POR: CS must track VCC at ramp) ✓.
- C78 100 nF @2.14 mm + C45 22 µF bulk satisfy DS decoupling; R52/R90-R92 trivial power.
- Bus sharing: MISO_FL→R92 0 Ω joins SPI_MISO — only one SPI slave CS active at a time, standard.
