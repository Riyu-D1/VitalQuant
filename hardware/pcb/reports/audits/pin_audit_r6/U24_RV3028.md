## Absolute-max vs power states
VDD −0.3…6 V; signal pins ≤VDD+0.3 — all on the +3V3 domain, 3.3 V ≤3.6 V ✓ every powered state. Flat: off. Skin-fault n/a. VBACKUP correctly grounded — no lithium-coin path exists, by design.

## Sequencing / passives
- RTC accepts VDD ramp any order (AppMan §3); I2C pull-ups share VDD.
- C37 100 nF @1.6 mm; R112/R115 10 kΩ trivial.

## Findings
- Carried-open item: RTC_INT → TP28 only (no MCU route). Not a new defect; FW workaround documented. R8 of layout: route blocked by validated electrode/power copper — unchanged.
- Otherwise clean: 8/8 pads verified against real App Manual detail.
