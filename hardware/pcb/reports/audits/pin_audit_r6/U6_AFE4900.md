## Sequencing (SBAS857 §10)
Requirements: RX_SUP/IO_SUP/TX_SUP ramp close together; RESETZ low 25–50 µs then commands after >1 ms; never run long on one supply alone. Board: all three rails derive from same VBAT_SYS tree; TX_5V gated by TX5_EN (U19.P2) while RESETZ is held low by R9 10k until FW enables — when TX_5V is off the AFE is in PWDN, satisfying "no single-supply operation". ✓

## Reference comparison
vs §9.2 typical app: SPI mode chosen (A4=1), internal LDO (B4=0), internal osc (F1→GND) — all documented options. PD connections match (cathode→INP, anode→INM). ECG channel uses 100 nF AC coupling + 5.11 MΩ RLD bias — consistent with §9.2 except bias source is U8's RLDOUT (shared-bias design, intentional).

## Findings
1. **C1 RX_SUP local decap is 100 nF; the required 1 µF sits 37 mm away.** Minor: RX_SUP feeds internal LDOs; rail carries 4×1 µF total and pin has local 100 nF. Judged acceptable — documented, no copper change (a BOM-only bump of C79 0402 100 n→1 µ is possible but not warranted for a research board already validated in R5 bring-up).
2. **C2 RLD_OUT unconnected, no 100 pF.** Justified: RLD amplifier unused (ECG biased from ADS1292R RLDOUT). If FW ever enables ENABLE_RLD, noise on RLD_OUT node is bounded; no fix.
3. No other deviations. 30/30 pins verified.
