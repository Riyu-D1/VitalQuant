*Note on pin 2: /OE driven by CS_MAX86178 (3.3 V domain) — VI recommended max 3.6 V / abs 4.2 V per §5.1/5.4, so a 3.3 V level on a VCCA-referenced control pin is **explicitly legal**. Active-low OE means the translator is enabled whenever the MCU does NOT assert CS_MAX86178... check: net name CS_MAX86178 doubles as U26 enable — when MCU selects the MAX86141 (CS low) OE=low→enabled→MISO passes; when CS high, translator Hi-Z. Deliberate design tying translation-enable to slave-select. Verified consistent.

## Absolute-max vs power states
- Battery-only/USB-only/charging: VCCA=1.8 V, VCCB=3.3 V, A1=1.8 V logic, B1→MCU 3.3 V; control pins ≤3.3 V ≤3.6/4.2 V ✓.
- Partial power: VCCA=0 while VCCB=3.3 V (1V8 LDO off) → Ioff Hi-Z ports per §5.7; A1 back-feed impossible ✓. Reverse case same.
- Flat: all off. Skin-fault n/a.

## Sequencing / passives
- SN74AXC supports any VCCA/VCCB ramp order (axc feature) ✓.
- C90/C91 100 nF @2.2–2.7 mm per TI decoupling guidance; R117 10k default-high OE.

None. 10/10 pads verified; previously undocumented control-input rating now confirmed legal.
