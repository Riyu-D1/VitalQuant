## Absolute-max vs power states
VDD overvolt limit 5 V; SDA/SCL rated ≤3.6 V leakage-tested; ADDR ≤VDD+0.6. Actuals: VDD 3.3 V; I2C pins ≤1.8 V in all powered states; ADDR 0 V. Battery-flat: rails off together. Skin-fault: sensor reads skin through IR — no electrical contact, n/a.

## Sequencing / passives
- VDD 3.3 V may exceed V_I2C 1.8 V ramp order — DS permits: SDA/SCL are fail-safe to 3.6 V independent of V_I2C ramp (leakage spec covers it).
- C61 100 nF @0.39 mm — well inside the 10 mm requirement. Pull-ups 2.2 kΩ → I²C std-mode rise time fine at 1.8 V.

## Findings
None. Variant/rail pairing confirmed correct (a 3V3-I2C variant mis-mounted would have been a real defect — it is not). 6/6 pads verified.
