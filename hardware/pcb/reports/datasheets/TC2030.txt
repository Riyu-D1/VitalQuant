      Tag-Connect - Save Cost and Space on Every Board!
The TC2050-CTX and TC2030-CTL-NL are Plug-of-Nails™ Spring-Pin cables intended for ARM CORTEX SWD (Single
Wire Debug) applications. Our patented Plug-of-Nails™ connectors plug directly to a tiny footprint of pads and
locating holes in your PCB saving you cost and PCB space. The cable’s ribbon connector plugs into the standard
FTSH-105 style 10-pin Cortex Debug Connector as found in KEIL’s ULINK2 and other CORTEX compatible debuggers.
