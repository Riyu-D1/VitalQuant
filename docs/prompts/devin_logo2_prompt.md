## Concepts: at least 6, from these seeds plus your own researched ideas
1. R-wave V: the sharp QRS spike forms the "V" of Vital, so the name starts with a heartbeat.
2. Negative-space pulse: an ECG spike or heart hidden in the counter of the Q, or in the gap between letters.
3. Quantised heartbeat: a PQRST traced by discrete sample dots or steps, meaning "life, measured". It must read clearly as a heartbeat, NOT as telecom or signal bars.
4. Breath plus beat: one continuous line where a slow respiration wave envelopes the ECG (respiratory sinus arrhythmia), so two vitals live in one stroke.
5. Vectorcardiogram loop: the heart's electrical-axis loop (VCG) as an organic mark. It's a niche cardiology reference, and could form a Q.
6. Body-to-hub: a pulse line that turns into a wireless arc or data, meaning continuous monitoring from chest to clinician.
7. Chest-lead placement: an abstract torso or rib shape with the device at the chest site, where that dot doubles as the dot (tittle) of the "i" in Vital.
